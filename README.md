# Truck Bans

### Can trucks drive today? Know before you dispatch.

Truck Bans is a fast, driver-first checker for truck driving bans across Europe: Sunday and public-holiday bans, the German summer Saturday ban, night bans and the Ukrainian heat ban, with the times, weight limits, fines and exemptions you need.

## Features

- **Bans active now**: country, end time and minimum weight at a glance
- **Next-ban countdown**: see what starts next and when
- **14-day ban calendar** to plan around every ban day
- **Fines and exemptions per country**, including who is exempt
- **Germany**: Sunday and holiday ban, summer Saturday ban with a map of the affected motorway stretches
- **Ukraine**: heat ban per region
- **Truck-ban news**: rule changes, holidays and closures
- **Follow only your countries**; dark and light themes, works offline, 20+ languages
